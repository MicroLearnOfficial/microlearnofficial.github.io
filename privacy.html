Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 5, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 5, 2026

Welcome to MicroLearn. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect and share your personal information when you use the MicroLearn mobile application ("App", "Service"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

While using MicroLearn, we may collect the following categories of information:

a) Account Information

  • Email address and profile information (via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication)
  • Username and profile avatar

b) App Usage Data

  • Learning progress, completed lessons and quiz results
  • Achievements (badges), daily streaks and favorites

c) Technical Data

  • Device information (model, operating system version, language setting)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data
  • In-app analytics and usage statistics

d) Advertising Identifiers

  • Advertising ID used by Google AdMob

e) Purchase Records

  • In-app purchase records processed by Google Play Billing

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account and manage your session securely
  • Synchronize your learning progress across devices
  • Personalize content based on your interests
  • Analyze and improve app quality
  • Detect and fix errors
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Process optional in-app purchases
  • Provide user support

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

MicroLearn may display advertisements through Google AdMob to support the app's revenue. AdMob may use data such as your advertising identifier and device information to show you more relevant ads. To learn more about how this data is processed, or to opt out of personalized ads, you can use your device's ad settings (on Android: Settings > Google > Ads) or visit Google's Ads Policies page.

5. Why We Show Ads

Advertisements help support the continued development of MicroLearn and allow us to keep many features available free of charge to our users.

6. In-App Purchases

MicroLearn may offer optional premium features or content. These purchases are securely processed by Google Play Billing. MicroLearn never directly sees, processes or stores your credit card or payment information; all payment transactions are handled through Google's secure infrastructure.

7. Signing In with Google

If you choose to sign in with Google, we only receive the basic profile information necessary to create and verify your account (name, email address, profile picture) from Google. We do not have access to your Google account password.

8. App Permissions

MicroLearn may request limited permissions required for specific features, such as internet access, notifications, or authentication services. Permissions are requested only when necessary.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We only share information when:

Your personal data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the Service and comply with our legal obligations. When you delete your account, associated data is removed from our systems or anonymized within a reasonable period.
